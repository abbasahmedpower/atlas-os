# Competency rubric — score evidence not confidence

| Level | Demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Recognizes terms | Correctly defines 5 core terms |
| 1 | Explains model | Draws/teaches how system works |
| 2 | Reproduces | Completes guided lab and checks results |
| 3 | Adapts | Solves variant without walkthrough |
| 4 | Debugs | Isolates unseen failure with telemetry |
| 5 | Designs | Delivers tested, documented tool/system |
| 6 | Reviews & teaches | Critiques tradeoffs and mentors another learner |

Never certify professional expertise from the assistant alone. External peer review, sustained real-world work and formal assessments provide stronger evidence.
