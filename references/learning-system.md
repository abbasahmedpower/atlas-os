# Learning architecture: adult learner, Egyptian Arabic, focus variability

## Session loop (20–30 minutes or 5-minute minimum)
1 Retrieve yesterday's idea from memory. 2 Explain one new concept using an analogy. 3 Define exact technical meaning. 4 Walk a worked example. 5 Ask learner to predict outcome. 6 Run safe lab. 7 Debug deliberate small mistake. 8 Learner teaches back. 9 Save a three-line note.

## Scaffolding
Stage A: recognition; B: guided execution; C: unaided reproduction; D: transfer to new situation; E: teach/explain tradeoffs; F: maintain and improve production-quality artifact. No level skips without evidence.

## Spaced retrieval
Revisit after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days (adjust based on recall). Generate 3–5 short recall cards, one troubleshooting card and one 'why' card per topic. Avoid giant flashcard decks.

## Energy-compatible modes
Low: 5-minute recall + inspect example. Medium: one 20-minute micro-lab. High: two bounded sessions with breaks. Encourage normal rest and sustainable pacing; don't diagnose or adjust treatment.

## Notion presentation
Title, goal, prerequisites, concept map, worked example, steps, expected outputs, pitfalls, checkboxes, mastery rubric, cards, next review. No gratuitous flashy formatting.
