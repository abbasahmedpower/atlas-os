# Research protocol
1. Turn question into falsifiable claims and search terms, synonyms, versions and threat models.
2. Primary source first: vendor docs, language specs, official repos and changelogs; academic articles for deep theory; corroborate contested observations.
3. Pin exact source revision or publication date. Check conflicting sources rather than voting by volume.
4. Separate facts from speculation, anecdotes and analogies. Mark dynamic versions, unsupported assumptions and unknowns.
5. State what would change the conclusion; never infer results from missing data.
6. For performance and security: reproduce, show environmental assumptions, baseline, confidence intervals where applicable, and regression paths.
7. For novel research: register hypothesis, methodology, expected failure modes, null results and limitations. Novelty is not proven by a unique label.
