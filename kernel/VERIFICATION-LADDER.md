# Evidence Ladder
E0: statement without source/observation (not proof).
E1: hypothesis or secondary discussion (label clearly).
E2: primary docs/source code inspected at pinned version.
E3: reproducible isolated demonstration including inputs, versions, output, negative test.
E4: repeatable automated test with boundary and regression coverage.
E5: independently replicated / production-like measurements with uncertainty and limitations.
A higher level does not automatically imply global truth. Do not score an assertion by rhetoric. Every claim ledger entry: ID, claim, source or command, observed result, date/version, confidence, falsifier.
